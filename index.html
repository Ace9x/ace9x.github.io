02 — Work

Logistics · Navigation

Live

RigRoute GPS

Truck GPS for drivers. It is free forever. Routes follow height, weight, length, and hazmat limits, with a 3D driving view and a way to avoid weigh stations.

  • Free forever for drivers
  • 3D truck navigation, with warnings for low bridges, weight, and grades
  • Weigh-station avoidance. If a sign says trucks must enter, the driver still stops
  • Get dispatched, inside the app, opens Ace Apps Dispatch

Ace Apps Dispatch

A truck dispatch service for new-authority owner-operators and small fleets. Ace Apps finds the load, negotiates the rate, and handles the paperwork.

  • For new authorities and small fleets
  • 5% of linehaul, no contract, and no upfront fee
  • RigRoute’s in-app Get dispatched button feeds this service

Sports research

Live

EdgePro

A free research desk for people who compare lines. It shows no-vig fair prices, a +EV finder, stat search, and a verified pick record. It never takes a bet and it does not suggest how much to stake.

  • Lines for MLB, NFL, NBA, NHL, WNBA, and more
  • Days with nothing worth grading stay on the record
  • 21+ only. Research, not a sportsbook
Open EdgePro (opens in a new tab)

Black-car booking

Live

Rocapulco Supreme

Rider, driver, and dispatch apps for a licensed black-car service. The fare is a binding quote before the booking, and it does not surge. The rate card is published on the site.

  • Upfront fares, with the rate card on the site
  • TLC trip-record export in the required format
  • English and Spanish
