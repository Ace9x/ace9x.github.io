Logistics · Navigation

RigRoute GPS

Truck GPS that routes around low bridges, weight limits and hazmat restrictions, with car, cargo van and reefer modes. The dispatcher console runs the fleet: live map, load board, drag-to-assign, proof of delivery, detention timers, invoices and PDF rate-con import.

  • Truck-legal routing by height, weight and hazmat class
  • Hours-of-service clock, truck stops, weather on the route
  • Dispatcher console with fleets kept separate by database rules
  • Screens lock against tapping above 5 mph; SOS always works

Sports data · Research

EdgePro

A research desk for MLB, NFL, NBA, NHL, WNBA and more. It grades lines from live prices and keeps an honest public record. It's a research tool, not a sportsbook: it never takes bets or suggests how much to stake.

  • Live prices graded A–D with the reasoning shown
  • Saying "nothing today" counts as a valid result
  • 21+ gate, state help lines, and no tracking of your location
