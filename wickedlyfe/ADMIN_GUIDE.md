# Wicked Lyfe — how to update the shop

Open the admin on your phone: [https://ace9x.github.io/wickedlyfe/admin/](https://ace9x.github.io/wickedlyfe/admin/)

Sign in with the email Ace Apps approved. You'll get a 6-digit code and a link. Type the code, or tap the link. Either one signs you in.

The public site does not show an email address or a home address. People reach the site team through **Talk to us**.

## Products

Tap **Products**.

- **Add item** starts a new piece.
- **Edit** changes the name, the price in dollars, the description, and the buy link.
- Leave the price blank to show **Coming soon**.
- Tap the size chips (S, M, L, XL, 2XL, One size). Add any other size in the box.
- **Sold out** puts a sold-out badge on the site.
- **Show on site** hides a piece when it's off. A draft from a gear note starts hidden.
- **Photos**: tap the file box, then take a picture or pick one from your gallery. Use **Up** and **Down** to set the first photo. **Remove** deletes one.
- **Up** and **Down** on the list change the order on the site. You can also drag a row.
- **Delete item** asks you to confirm.
- **Save** shows **Saved ✓**. Then tap **View site** to check it.

## Music

Tap **Music**.

- **Listen on all platforms** is the button at the top of the music page. Paste the UnitedMasters artist page, then **Save**.
- **Add release** adds a song or project. Fill in the title, the artist, the type, the date, and the Spotify, Apple Music, and UnitedMasters links.
- **Badge** is the line on the big music hero. The new EP uses **New EP out now**. Clear it when that release should look like the others.
- **Featured** is the release that opens first. The one with a badge opens first.
- **Artwork link** can be the cover from UnitedMasters, or tap **Or upload artwork** and pick a picture.
- **Add video** needs a YouTube link. The page embeds it from the private YouTube player and waits to load it until someone scrolls to it.
- **Show on site** hides a release when it's off.
- **Up** and **Down** change the order.
- **Delete** asks you to confirm.

New music shows up on the site after you save. You do not need a code change.

## Messages

Tap **Messages**. These are the notes from **Talk to us** on the site.

- Each note shows the name, the email they typed, the topic, the message, and a photo if they added one.
- Topics are **Add new gear**, **Site update**, **Idea**, and **Other**.
- The status is **new**, **in progress**, or **done**. It saves when you change it.
- **New gear request** starts a hidden product from that note. The name and description come from the message, and the photo is copied onto the product. It does not go on the shop until you turn on **Show on site** and save.
- Photos people upload stay private. Only a signed-in admin can open them.

## Brand

Tap **Brand** to change the logo, tagline, bio, the listen link, Instagram link, announcement, accent color, Stripe link, and PayPal link.

Leave the announcement blank to hide that bar. A logo you upload replaces the mark in the header and the big hero.

Stripe or PayPal links are the checkout buttons. A buy link on one piece overrides those for that piece. If neither is set, the button says **DM to order** and opens Instagram.

## Ask the assistant

Tap **Ask**. Type what you want, for example "add a red hoodie, $60" or "change the banner." Add a photo if it helps. Send it.

Your older notes stay on that screen with a status and any reply. The list refreshes about every 30 seconds.

Ace Apps' assistant reads these and makes the change, usually within a few hours.

## If something looks wrong

The message on screen is the one to follow. You don't need the technical details.

If it says your email isn't approved yet, text Ace Apps and ask them to add that email.
