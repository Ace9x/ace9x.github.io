# Wicked Lyfe — how to update the shop

Open the admin on your phone: [https://ace9x.github.io/wickedlyfe/admin/](https://ace9x.github.io/wickedlyfe/admin/)

Sign in with the email Ace Apps approved. You'll get a 6-digit code and a link. Type the code, or tap the link. Either one signs you in.

## Products

Tap **Products**.

- **Add item** starts a new piece.
- **Edit** changes the name, the price in dollars, the description, and the buy link.
- Leave the price blank to show **Coming soon**.
- Tap the size chips (S, M, L, XL, 2XL, One size). Add any other size in the box.
- **Sold out** puts a sold-out badge on the site.
- **Show on site** hides a piece when it's off.
- **Photos**: tap the file box, then take a picture or pick one from your gallery. Use **Up** and **Down** to set the first photo. **Remove** deletes one.
- **Up** and **Down** on the list change the order on the site. You can also drag a row.
- **Delete item** asks you to confirm.
- **Save** shows **Saved ✓**. Then tap **View site** to check it.

## Brand

Tap **Brand** to change the logo, tagline, bio, music link, Instagram link, announcement, accent color, Stripe link, PayPal link, and contact email.

Leave the announcement blank to hide that bar. A logo you upload replaces the mark in the header and the big hero.

Stripe or PayPal links are the checkout buttons. A buy link on one piece overrides those for that piece. If neither is set, the button says **DM to order** and opens Instagram.

## Ask the assistant

Tap **Ask**. Type what you want, for example "add a red hoodie, $60" or "change the banner." Add a photo if it helps. Send it.

Your older notes stay on that screen with a status and any reply. The list refreshes about every 30 seconds.

Ace Apps' assistant reads these and makes the change, usually within a few hours.

## If something looks wrong

The message on screen is the one to follow. You don't need the technical details.

If it says your email isn't approved yet, text Ace Apps and ask them to add that email.
